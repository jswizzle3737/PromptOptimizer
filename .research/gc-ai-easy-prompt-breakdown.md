## Easy Prompt (the "prompt improver")
- Launched Aug 12, 2024 (blog: /blog/product-update-easy-prompt)
- **Beta name was literally "Prompt Optimizer"** — renamed to "Easy Prompt"
- Promise: "Say what you need, Easy Prompt does the rest" — turns a one-line plain-language request into a "fully structured", "expertly-written artisan prompt applying the latest prompting techniques in seconds"
- Positioned as users' "favorite part of GC AI"

## Their prompting methodology (learn/how-to-prompt-legal-ai, glossary)
1. Give context, not just a task (role, perspective, what good looks like)
2. Ask for reasoning — exact quotes and citations
3. Work in small steps (decompose big tasks)
4. Reuse what works (save to skill library)
5. Keep a human in the loop
