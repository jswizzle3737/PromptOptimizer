What's the difference between the Level 101 and Level 201 classes?
Our 100-series courses are an introduction to using AI for legal work, suitable for beginners. Our 200-series courses are a deep dive into advanced techniques and require some familiarity with AI or completion of a 100-series course.
Can law firm lawyers take these classes, or are they only for in-house counsel?
While the courses are designed with in-house counsel in mind, law firm lawyers benefit from them too. Past participants include associates, partners, and paralegals across practice areas like patent law, litigation, and trademark prosecution.
Are the classes eligible for Continuing Legal Education (CLE) credit?
Courses 101, 201, 202, and 110 are eligible for California participatory MCLE credit. Courses 101 and 202 offer 1 hour in Technology in the Practice of Law; 201 offers 1.25 hours in Technology in the Practice of Law; and 110 offers 1 hour in Legal Ethics.
What tools or software will I be using during the classes?
The classes are taught using GC AI, a legal-specific AI platform. Participants receive a free trial of GC AI (14 days for individual courses, 30 days for the Custom class). GC AI also offers a Word add-in, giving participants hands-on experience with AI-powered document editing directly in Microsoft Word. While the course focuses on GC AI, the skills learned can be applied to general-purpose AI tools.
How can these classes benefit my legal work and productivity?
These classes teach you practical, immediately useful skills in AI for legal work. You'll learn how to get high-quality outputs from AI tools across drafting, reviewing, editing, and research. The skills compound as you build them into your daily workflows.
I'm new to AI - is that OK?
Yes. The courses assume you're a legal professional with basic tech skills (e.g., using a web browser) but do not assume any prior AI experience. If you can write an email, you can use AI for your legal work.
