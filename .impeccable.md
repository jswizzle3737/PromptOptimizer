# Design Context — PromptOptimizer

Established 2026-09-30 from a design critique session. Governs all future design work on this
extension.

## Users

Two overlapping groups, both already fluent with conversational AI:

- **The improver.** Uses ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini most days. Knows their prompts are
  underperforming but does not want to learn prompt-engineering frameworks. Wants a one-click
  upgrade without reading documentation.
- **The power user.** Wants a fast in-page accelerator: keyboard-first, no modal friction, and
  visibility into the system prompt that is doing the work.

Context of use: mid-task, inside someone else's web app, often with a draft already typed. The
tool is an interruption to their real work and must justify the interruption instantly.

## Brand personality

**Precise. Unobtrusive. Professional.**

The voice of a good instrument, not a good salesman. It states what it did and gets out of the
way. It does not celebrate routine actions, does not use exclamation marks, and does not
editorialise. When something degrades, it says so plainly rather than absorbing the failure.

Emotional goal: **confidence**. The user should trust that the output is what it claims to be.

## Aesthetic direction

**Neutral-professional** (chosen explicitly over a rebrand). Restrained, high-legibility, and
deliberately quiet — the interface lives inside a host page it does not own and must never fight
it for attention.

Ten designs are on offer (added 2026-10-02 at the owner's request, superseding the earlier
dark/light pair). Ink is the default and Paper its light counterpart; the other eight are
Terminal, Ember, Harbor, Contrast, Manuscript, Sand, Glacier and Meadow. Each must be a complete
design: its own palette AND a type, corner or weight decision, so the list expresses range rather
than recolouring. `unit/themes.test.mjs` enforces that, and `node check_contrast.js` holds all ten
to WCAG AA.

### Anti-references — must not look like this

The 2024–25 "AI SaaS" default, specifically:

- Inter (or Roboto / Arial / Open Sans) as the primary typeface
- Indigo / violet accent (`#6366F1` and neighbours) on near-black navy surfaces
- Colored glow shadows on interactive elements
- Gradient text
- Emoji used as iconography
- Glassmorphism / backdrop blur as decoration
- Every setting wrapped in an identical rounded card
- Elastic or overshoot easing
- A long theme list that is only recolours (every design must differ in type, shape or weight too)

## Design principles

1. **The framework is the product.** Framework selection gets the prominent, always-reachable
   control. Engine, theme, and history are secondary and belong in Options.
2. **Never report a degraded result as a success.** If the requested engine failed, say so, name
   the reason, and offer the fix. Silent fallback is a trust bug, not a graceful degradation.
3. **One name per action.** The product is "PromptOptimizer" — the verb is
   *optimize*, everywhere, in every surface.
4. **Every option must be visibly reachable.** No control may hide choices behind an affordance
   the user cannot see.
5. **Tokens or nothing.** All color, space, type, and motion resolve through `theme.css`. No
   surface hardcodes a value.
6. **Explain in place.** Help belongs next to the thing it explains, not on a settings page.
