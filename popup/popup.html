PromptOptimizer rewrites a rough draft into a structured, high-performance prompt. Three things worth knowing:

  1. 1
    Pick a framework Each one shapes the prompt differently. Smart Expand is a safe default.
  2. 2
    Pick an engine Chrome Nano runs on your device when your browser has it. Otherwise add a key from any provider (OpenRouter has free models); you can do it right here after this screen.
  3. 3
    Use it inside your AI chat On ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and similar sites a floating Optimize button appears beside the chat box. Alt+O or the right-click menu opens it on any page.
